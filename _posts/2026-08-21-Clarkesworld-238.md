A couple of good stories worth reading, and many I bounced off hard. Thomas Ha is the standout, I'll be looking for more of his writing. 

The Life and Times of the Extraterrestrial Potoo, K. J. Khan ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 

Touching story of friendship across species in the midst of colonial exploitation. While the prose is uncomplicated, the characters and their relationship are vivid.

Cliff's Girl, Fiona Moore ⭐⭐

Everybody is building and hacking robots while living in cosy post-climate-collapse pastoral communities. I just couldn't suspend my disbelief.

Focus, Carlos Hernandez ⭐⭐⭐

Twitchers with clairvoyant tech that lets them be one with the birds, are haunted by their dead friend.

The Animator and the Glazer, Ju Chu, translated by S. Qiouyi Lu ⭐⭐

Generative AI has ended white-collar work. Only those who glaze their style can hope to protect it. A fable-esque tale of a long con gone bad. 

Our Revels Now Are Ended, Thomas Ha ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 

A virtual interrogation doesn't go well when the tables are turned and the reality of the interrogator is questioned.

This is an Emergency Broadcast, R.S.A. Garcia ⭐

Constructed from fragmentary sentences from a mass media stream of consciousness. Social commentary rather than narrative and plot. DNF

Nesting Behavior of Imported Species Troglodytes aedon on Planet Americearth, R.L. Meza ⭐⭐

Told from the perspective of an avian xenomorph trying to oust human explorers from its forest. More an exercise in alien perspective than a story.
